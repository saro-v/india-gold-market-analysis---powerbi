India Gold Market Analysis

Gold Prices, Consumption, Demand & Silver Price Trends

Project at a glance

This two-page Power BI report connects market prices with gold consumption and demand across India. The first page is focused on prices. The second page moves into regional and state-level consumption.

Dashboard

Page 1 — Gold & Silver Market Pulse

Page 2 — India Gold Consumption & Demand

Key points

  • 22K gold and 999 silver price trends are shown for the selected period.
  • Gold rates are compared across 24K, 22K, 18K and 14K.
  • Gold consumption is compared across states and regions.
  • India gold demand is shown by Jewellery, Bar & Coin and Gold ETF.
  • Selected jeweller 22K rates are included for comparison.

Data and method

The project uses published bullion, retail and India demand observations where available. The state-wise consumption view is a derived analytical estimate based on published regional demand shares and state allocation weights.

Important: State-level values are not official state-wise statistics, and the dashboard is not a live market feed.
