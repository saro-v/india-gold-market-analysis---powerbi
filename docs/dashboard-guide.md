# Dashboard Guide

## Page 1 — Gold & Silver Market Pulse

**Purpose:** show what happened to gold and silver prices during the selected period.

Main visuals:

- Gold 22K KPI
- Silver 999 KPI
- Gold 22K price trend
- Silver 999 price trend
- Gold rate by purity
- Date range slicer
- PM-session filter

The page is mainly about **price movement**.

## Page 2 — India Gold Consumption & Demand

**Purpose:** show where gold consumption is concentrated and how demand differs by region and segment.

Main visuals:

- Total Gold Consumption
- Top Consuming State
- Leading Region Share
- Region selector
- State-wise Gold Consumption map
- Top States ranking
- Gold Consumption by Region
- Gold Demand by Segment
- 22K Gold Rate by Jeweller

The page is mainly about **consumption and demand**.

## Suggested presentation order

1. Start with the two headline rates on Page 1.
2. Point out the gold and silver trend lines.
3. Use the purity chart to explain the difference between karat levels.
4. Move to Page 2 and introduce the consumption view.
5. Start with the top-state ranking, then use the region selector.
6. Use the donut to explain the regional split.
7. Explain the state-estimate limitation before interpreting the map.
8. Finish with demand segments and jeweller rates.
