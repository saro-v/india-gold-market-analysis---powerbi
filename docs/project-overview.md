# Project Overview

## Title

**India Gold Market Analysis | Power BI**

## What the project does

This report brings together gold and silver prices, gold demand and state-wise gold consumption in one Power BI dashboard.

The report was kept to two pages so that the story is easy to follow during a presentation:

- **Page 1 — Gold & Silver Market Pulse:** price movement and gold purity comparison.
- **Page 2 — India Gold Consumption & Demand:** state, region, demand segment and jeweller comparison.

## Objectives

1. Show the latest published gold and silver rates available in the dataset.
2. Compare gold rates across major purity levels.
3. Show the selected-period movement of 22K gold and 999 silver.
4. Compare gold demand by major segment.
5. Compare estimated gold consumption across Indian states and regions.
6. Include selected 22K jeweller rates for context.
7. Keep the report simple enough to use interactively in a classroom presentation.

## Design

The dashboard uses a cream, white, gold and dark-blue palette. The background is intentionally subtle so that the charts remain the main focus.

## Important data point

The state-level consumption view is an analytical estimate. It is based on published regional demand information and state allocation weights because a current official state-by-state consumption series was not available in the required format.

This is stated clearly in the dashboard documentation rather than treating the estimate as an official statistic.
