# Short Presentation Notes

These are written as talking points rather than a script.

## Opening

“This is my Power BI analysis of the Indian gold market, with supporting silver price trends. I kept the report to two pages — the first page is about prices, and the second is about consumption and demand.”

## Page 1

“The first two cards show the selected 22K gold and 999 silver rates. The line charts show how they moved over the selected dates. The purity chart gives a quick comparison across 24K, 22K, 18K and 14K.”

## Page 2

“Here I move from price to consumption. The cards show total estimated consumption, the top state and the leading region. I can use the region selector to change the view.”

“The ranking is useful for seeing the leading states, while the donut gives a quick regional split. The map gives the geographic view.”

## Important clarification

“The state-wise consumption values are estimates, not official state-level statistics. They were derived from published regional demand shares and state allocation weights because a current official state series was not available in the required format.”

## Closing

“The main idea of the project is to connect market prices with consumption patterns in one simple, interactive report.”
